Connect Canvas

Canvas can hand you a file with every assignment and due date in it. Load it here once and the Due tab fills itself in. It stays on this phone, and no password or login is involved.
1 Open Canvas and go to Calendar.
2 Scroll down the right side and tap Calendar Feed.
3 Copy the address it shows. Change webcal:// at the front to https://, then open it. Your phone saves a .ics file.
4 Come back here and load that file.
